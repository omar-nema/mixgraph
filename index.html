B2B: Curated Music Discovery

Back2Back is a music discovery tool built from DJ set tracklists.

I find that algorithmic music discovery tools don't end up exposing me to new sounds. More and more I rely on DJ sets to find new music. With B2B, my goal is to continue to discover music through curators, but in a more immediate way.

Here's how it works: B2B shows you a random track. The songs that are directly connected to it are songs played right before, or right after in a DJ set. Hit shuffle until you find a track you like, and explore the adjacent songs :)

Mixes are sourced from Lot Radio and NTS Radio.

- Omar
