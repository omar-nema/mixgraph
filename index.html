back2back is a music discovery tool built from DJ set tracklists

I find that algorithmic music discovery tools don't end up exposing me to new sounds. More and more I rely on DJ sets to find new music. With B2B, my goal is to continue to discover music through curators, but in a more immediate way.

back2back — dig

In Dig mode, pan around until an album cover catches your eye

Dig lays out clusters of connected tracks as a wall of artwork, like a record shop. Drift through it like flipping through crates, then click anything that pulls you in.

back2back — shuffle

In Shuffle, let the DJs do the recommending

Shuffle drops you on a random track surrounded by songs DJs played right before or after it.

If you like the main song, the adjacent ones are recommendations driven by DJ sets. And if you don't, use the filters to zone in on spaces you're interested in.
